# Template: Weekly Sales Call Dossier

**Use this template:** End of day Friday or beginning of day Monday to review the prior week's customer calls for Grayson and Barron.

**Output location:** `customer-calls/weekly-briefs/`
**Output filename format:** `YYYY-MM-DD_to_YYYY-MM-DD_[person]_sales_calls.md`
Example: `2026-05-18_to_2026-05-27_grayson_sales_calls.md`

---

## Step 1: Sync call notes from GitHub

First, check for any new call notes that haven't been downloaded yet.

> "Check the heyito/Customer-Call-Notes GitHub repo for any new Grayson Cooper calls and Barron Caster calls added since [most recent file date in local folder]. Download any new files to the appropriate local folder in customer-calls/granola_notes/ without overwriting existing files."

---

## Step 2: Identify calls from the past week

Look at files in:
- `customer-calls/granola_notes/Grayson Cooper calls/`
- `customer-calls/granola_notes/Barron Caster calls/`

Filter to calls dated within the review window (Monday through Friday of the prior week, or Monday through Sunday depending on when you're running this).

**For companies with multiple calls in the window:** group them together under one company section.
**For companies with earlier calls in prior weeks:** read those earlier calls too if relevant context is needed (e.g., first pilot call helps understand what was discussed in a kickoff call).

---

## Step 3: Read the calls and generate the dossier

For each person (Grayson, Barron), produce a dossier with the following structure:

---

# [Person] Sales Calls: Week of [Date Range]

**Period:** [Monday date] through [Friday date], [Year]
**Compiled:** [Date]
**Source:** `customer-calls/granola_notes/[Person] calls/`

---

## [Company Name]

**Call date(s):** [Date] (if single call) OR [Date 1], [Date 2] (if multiple)
**Contact(s):** [Name], [Title/Role]

### Current Status
*(2 bullets)*
- Are they in a pilot? Pre-pilot? Getting qualified? Being disqualified? Explain the stage.
- What are the immediate next steps and timeline?

### Fit Assessment
- **Good fit:** [2-4 bullet points on what makes them a strong fit based on what came up in the call]
- **Concerns:** [2-4 bullet points on why they might not be a fit, or what could slow/block the deal]

### PM Insights
*(2-3 bullets on anything relevant to product: feature requests, friction, feedback on output quality, competitive mentions, workflow patterns, or anything that would inform roadmap decisions)*

---

*(Repeat for each company)*

---

## Step 4: Review checklist before saving

- [ ] Every company from the week is represented
- [ ] Companies with multiple calls in the window are grouped together, not listed separately
- [ ] Dates are accurate and listed in the header of each section
- [ ] Status bullets clearly state pilot stage (not started / shadow mode / active pilot / decision pending / passed / churned)
- [ ] PM insights are specific, not generic ("they want the product to be better" is not a useful PM insight)
- [ ] No em dashes used anywhere in the document

---

## Notes on running this for Barron's calls

Barron's calls tend to be a mix of:
- New prospect/demo calls (similar structure to Grayson's)
- Deep-dive sessions with existing customers like TrueMed (Adam Hood)
- Investor or partner calls

For **existing customer calls**, the status section should focus on what changed or what new feedback/requests were surfaced since the last call, not re-explaining what Ito is.

For **investor or partner calls**, skip this template and use separate notes if needed - those don't belong in the weekly dossier.

---

## Tips for making this more useful over time

- If the same piece of feedback comes up across two or more companies in a single week, flag it explicitly as a pattern at the top of the dossier.
- If a company drops out or disqualifies during the week, still include them with a brief note on why - patterns in loss reasons are valuable.
- Link to the individual call notes files at the top of each company section for easy reference.
- If there are strong customer quotes relevant to the output agent, PR comments, or other active work areas, flag them for the team rather than just burying them in the dossier.
